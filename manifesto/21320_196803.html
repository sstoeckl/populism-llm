WarningHow to read this page

The scores are holistic judgements. Each model read the whole manifesto text (in chunks of up to 20,000 tokens) and rated every dimension from the text as a whole. The passages quoted further down are examples the model cited, not the basis of the score: a score that rests on two paragraphs cannot be reduced to a few words, and a quoted passage alone will often look too weak, or too strong, for the number next to it. Read the models’ own explanations first; they state the reasoning behind each block of scores. The quotes then show where in the text the models looked.

Populism, populist ideology and liberalism

Thirteen dimensions scored on 0–10 by three model families from the full manifesto text, each with a verbatim evidence quote. Scores are aggregated as in Rode & Stöckl (2026): a chunk counts if the model marks its evidence as verified and the quote is found verbatim in the manifesto chunk (and is not copied from the prompt); confidence-weighted mean within model, plain mean across models.

What the models say about this manifesto

Each model’s own explanation of its scores, one per block of dimensions, written together with the scores.

Populism

Sonnet

The PSB manifesto attacks the governing PSC-PLP coalition as opportunists and demagogues who led Belgium to failure, invoking the nation's working people as the virtuous beneficiaries of socialist solutions, but the rhetoric is primarily programmatic rather than deeply Manichean or conspiratorial, yielding a moderate populism score.

GPT-4.1-mini

The manifesto criticizes political elites as opportunistic and demagogic while emphasizing a plan involving all forces of the nation, showing moderate anti-elitism and people-centrism with some moralized critique of the establishment.

Gemini

The text contains mild anti-elitist rhetoric targeting the ruling parties as opportunistic and demagogic, but lacks a moralized good-versus-evil framing or a strong invocation of a unified, virtuous 'people'.

Populist ideology

Sonnet

The manifesto is clearly left-populist: it champions full employment, economic planning, redistribution, social security expansion, and workers' rights, while criticising private financial groups and conservative elites; there is no nativist or right-populist content.

GPT-4.1-mini

The text presents a left-leaning populist ideology focused on social solutions, national unity, and broad political agreement, rejecting authoritarianism and emphasizing democratic reform.

Gemini

The manifesto exhibits a strong left-wing populist flavor, advocating for state intervention, public investment, and planning to counter the failures of private financial groups.

Liberalism

Sonnet

The manifesto shows mixed liberalism: it supports gender equality, minority protections, and parliamentary strengthening (pro-liberal), but also advocates state economic planning, public investment societies substituting for private capital, and coordinated capital markets (moderately anti-liberal economically), yielding a middling overall score.

GPT-4.1-mini

The manifesto supports democratic representation, equal rights, social promotion, economic planning, and coordination of financial institutions, reflecting a pro-liberal stance overall.

Gemini

The manifesto strongly supports political and social liberalism through constitutional reforms and gender equality, but rejects economic and financial liberalism in favor of state planning and capital market coordination.
