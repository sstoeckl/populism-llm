WarningHow to read this page

The scores are holistic judgements. Each model read the whole manifesto text (in chunks of up to 20,000 tokens) and rated every dimension from the text as a whole. The passages quoted further down are examples the model cited, not the basis of the score: a score that rests on two paragraphs cannot be reduced to a few words, and a quoted passage alone will often look too weak, or too strong, for the number next to it. Read the models’ own explanations first; they state the reasoning behind each block of scores. The quotes then show where in the text the models looked.

Populism, populist ideology and liberalism

Thirteen dimensions scored on 0–10 by three model families from the full manifesto text, each with a verbatim evidence quote. Scores are aggregated as in Rode & Stöckl (2026): a chunk counts if the model marks its evidence as verified and the quote is found verbatim in the manifesto chunk (and is not copied from the prompt); confidence-weighted mean within model, plain mean across models.

What the models say about this manifesto

Each model’s own explanation of its scores, one per block of dimensions, written together with the scores.

Populism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto invokes the people's collective aspirations and national history as a moral foundation, and frames opponents as either callous individualists or nostalgic statists, but stops well short of a sharp elite-vs-people dichotomy or conspiratorial framing, yielding only mild populist signals.

chunk 2 · The text is a technocratic policy manifesto with virtually no populist framing: references to "the people" are incidental, there is no sustained elite-bashing, and the single quasi-Manichean phrase ("trampa que nos tienden") is a mild rhetorical device rather than a moralized good-vs-evil narrative.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto expresses moderate anti-elitism by criticizing those who block fundamental projects and impose dogmas, while emphasizing a unifying people-centrism invoking fraternity and shared responsibility, and a mild manichean framing of 'wild capitalism' condemned by spiritual humanism.

chunk 2 · No evidence of populist rhetoric or themes is present in the text; it focuses on economic, infrastructural, environmental, and international policy without anti-elitism or people-centric appeals.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text exhibits very low populism, only invoking the concept of the people as a unified, virtuous group working toward national achievements without any anti-elitist or Manichean framing.

chunk 2 · The text contains no populist rhetoric, anti-elitist framing, or moralized people-versus-elite divisions.

Populist ideology

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The document blends center-left social democratic themes—poverty reduction, strong public services, workers' rights—with explicit support for private enterprise, free trade, and foreign investment, placing it primarily in a moderate left-populist ideological space with centrist pragmatic elements.

chunk 2 · The manifesto blends mild centre-left social concerns (reducing inequality, universal access) with strong pro-market, pro-competition, and pro-investment stances, placing it in a moderate social-democratic/centrist space with no strong populist ideological flavour.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The text combines centrist populist appeals to making politics possible and realizing shared dreams with left-leaning emphasis on social programs and poverty reduction, but lacks right-wing nationalist or anti-immigration rhetoric.

chunk 2 · The text lacks any populist ideological content, neither left, right, nor centrist; it is a technocratic and policy-driven document.

Gemini

chunk 1 · There is no evidence of a distinct populist ideology, as the text lacks the core populist antagonism between the pure people and the corrupt elite.

chunk 2 · There is no evidence of populist ideology as the text focuses on institutional, technocratic, and programmatic policy proposals.

Liberalism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto consistently supports democratic institutions, rule of law, gender equality, and market competition alongside state regulation, reflecting a mixed but broadly pro-liberal stance across political, social, economic, and financial dimensions.

chunk 2 · The manifesto consistently endorses democratic governance, human rights, free-market competition, capital market development, prudent monetary policy, and open investment attraction, reflecting a broadly liberal orientation across all four sub-dimensions.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto strongly supports liberal political institutions like democracy and dialogue, social protections for vulnerable groups, economic policies balancing private enterprise with public regulation, and prudent financial policies fostering growth and employment.

chunk 2 · The manifesto explicitly supports democracy, human rights, market competition, and financial system prudential oversight, indicating a strong pro-liberal stance.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text strongly supports classical liberal principles, advocating for democracy, private enterprise, and social protections, alongside state regulation to ensure market stability.

chunk 2 · The text strongly supports classical liberal principles, including market competition, democratic governance, human rights, and the development of capital markets.
