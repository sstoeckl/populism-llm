WarningHow to read this page

The scores are holistic judgements. Each model read the whole manifesto text (in chunks of up to 20,000 tokens) and rated every dimension from the text as a whole. The passages quoted further down are examples the model cited, not the basis of the score: a score that rests on two paragraphs cannot be reduced to a few words, and a quoted passage alone will often look too weak, or too strong, for the number next to it. Read the models’ own explanations first; they state the reasoning behind each block of scores. The quotes then show where in the text the models looked.

Populism, populist ideology and liberalism

Thirteen dimensions scored on 0–10 by three model families from the full manifesto text, each with a verbatim evidence quote. Scores are aggregated as in Rode & Stöckl (2026): a chunk counts if the model marks its evidence as verified and the quote is found verbatim in the manifesto chunk (and is not copied from the prompt); confidence-weighted mean within model, plain mean across models.

What the models say about this manifesto

Each model’s own explanation of its scores, one per block of dimensions, written together with the scores.

Populism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto shows mild populist tendencies: it critiques global capital escaping democratic control and opposes authoritarianism, but frames these concerns in technocratic, social-democratic language rather than a sharp people-vs-elite narrative, and lacks strong Manichean or people-centric rhetoric.

chunk 2 · The text shows only mild populist signals: a brief reference to overcoming 'polyarchy' and concern for individuals in the community, but no sustained people-vs-elite framing, no moralized good-vs-evil narrative, and no invocation of a unified virtuous 'people' against corrupt elites.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto critiques global capital as detached from democratic control and highlights the role of peoples and nations, reflecting moderate anti-elitism, people-centrism, and moralized conflict in globalization.

chunk 2 · The manifesto contains no explicit references to populist themes such as anti-elitism, people-centrism, or Manichean moral struggles, indicating absence of populism.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text contains no populist rhetoric, anti-elitist framing, or moralized good-versus-evil divisions between the people and elites.

chunk 2 · The text contains no populist rhetoric, anti-elitist framing, or moralized good-versus-evil divisions.

Populist ideology

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto is clearly left-leaning social-democratic, emphasizing workers' interests, redistribution, social solidarity, and state intervention, with only a minor nod to national identity (ethnic Hungarians abroad) and no significant right-populist or centrist-populist framing.

chunk 2 · The manifesto is primarily social-democratic left-leaning, emphasizing support for disadvantaged groups, redistribution, and social rights, with only a minor nod to national identity (citizenship for Hungarians abroad) and a bridging centrist role; overall populist ideological intensity is low.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The text emphasizes left-leaning populist themes like worker representation and democratic control over globalization, with some centrist appeals to pluralism and national cohesion, and minor right-leaning cultural identity references.

chunk 2 · There is no evidence in the text of populist ideological stances, whether left, right, or centrist; the manifesto focuses on social democratic and internationalist policies without populist framing.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text does not exhibit populist ideology, focusing instead on standard social democratic and institutional policy proposals.

chunk 2 · There is no evidence of populist ideology, as the text focuses on standard social democratic and internationalist policy positions.

Liberalism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto is broadly pro-liberal in political and social dimensions (rule of law, minority rights, anti-discrimination, democratic pluralism), moderately pro-liberal economically (tax reduction, market competition, foreign investment), but mixed on financial markets (calling for effective control of financial flows alongside market-based development).

chunk 2 · The manifesto is strongly pro-liberal on political and social dimensions (democracy, minority rights, rule of law, gender equality), moderately liberal economically (private capital, decentralized taxation), and mixed on financial markets (endorsing 'effective control' of financial processes alongside openness to international capital).

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto strongly supports democratic institutions, human rights, social equality, and economic growth with state involvement, but lacks explicit discussion of financial market liberalization.

chunk 2 · The manifesto explicitly supports democracy, human rights, social equality including women's rights, market-oriented economic development with private capital involvement, and prudent financial oversight, demonstrating a strong liberal orientation.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text strongly supports political and social liberalism through democratic institutions and minority rights, while advocating a mixed social-market economy.

chunk 2 · The text strongly supports political democracy, minority rights, and international cooperation, while advocating for a regulated market economy that incorporates private capital alongside state oversight.
