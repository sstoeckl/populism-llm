WarningHow to read this page

The scores are holistic judgements. Each model read the whole manifesto text (in chunks of up to 20,000 tokens) and rated every dimension from the text as a whole. The passages quoted further down are examples the model cited, not the basis of the score: a score that rests on two paragraphs cannot be reduced to a few words, and a quoted passage alone will often look too weak, or too strong, for the number next to it. Read the models’ own explanations first; they state the reasoning behind each block of scores. The quotes then show where in the text the models looked.

Populism, populist ideology and liberalism

Thirteen dimensions scored on 0–10 by three model families from the full manifesto text, each with a verbatim evidence quote. Scores are aggregated as in Rode & Stöckl (2026): a chunk counts if the model marks its evidence as verified and the quote is found verbatim in the manifesto chunk (and is not copied from the prompt); confidence-weighted mean within model, plain mean across models.

What the models say about this manifesto

Each model’s own explanation of its scores, one per block of dimensions, written together with the scores.

Populism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto explicitly distances itself from populism ("rozwiązania odważne, ale nie populistyczne"), while criticising ruling elites (PiS, PO) in moderately sharp terms and invoking citizen welfare, yielding a low-to-moderate populism score overall.

chunk 2 · The manifesto explicitly rejects populism ("Porzucimy populistyczną politykę"), while showing only mild anti-elite rhetoric focused on corruption and nepotism of the current government, without a strong Manichean or people-centric populist frame.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto expresses moderate anti-elitism through criticism of established parties and their governance failures, emphasizes people-centrism by framing Poland as a community of free citizens with equal rights, and shows some moralized conflict against oppressive systems, resulting in a balanced populism score.

chunk 2 · The manifesto explicitly criticizes political nepotism and corruption, appeals to the wisdom of local communities, and opposes moral decay, indicating moderate anti-elitism, people-centrism, and some moral polarization.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text contains very low-level populist rhetoric, acknowledging citizens' distrust of political elites and framing the ruling party as a bad authority, but explicitly distances itself from populism.

chunk 2 · The text exhibits very low populism, explicitly rejecting populist economic policies while focusing on technocratic, institutional reforms and anti-nepotism.

Populist ideology

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The party's programmatic stance is centrist-liberal: it rejects both left and right extremes, promotes free markets and individual rights, and frames its appeal in technocratic good-governance terms rather than strong left-wing redistribution or right-wing nationalist identity politics.

chunk 2 · The manifesto's populist-adjacent elements are centrist-technocratic (good governance, citizen participation) rather than left or right populist; it explicitly favours privatisation, free markets, and EU integration, ruling out strong left or right populist ideology.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The text contains mild left-leaning populist elements in calls for removing privileges and simplifying taxes, weak right-leaning populism in rejecting nationalism and xenophobia, and centrist populism in appeals to collective responsibility and unity, reflecting a moderate overall populist ideology.

chunk 2 · The text rejects populist economic and social policies while emphasizing local community empowerment and balanced governance, reflecting a centrist-leaning populist ideology with mild left and right elements.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The manifesto rejects traditional left-right populist divisions, presenting a centrist technocratic alternative that acknowledges elite distrust without adopting a radical populist ideology.

chunk 2 · The text lacks left-wing or right-wing populist ideology, instead advocating for centrist, market-oriented, and liberal democratic governance.

Liberalism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto is strongly liberal across all four dimensions: it champions rule of law and constitutional courts (political), gender/LGBTQ+ equality and minority dignity (social), free markets, deregulation and privatisation (economic), and capital-market development including venture capital (financial).

chunk 2 · The manifesto is strongly liberal across all four dimensions: it champions judicial independence and rule of law, civil rights and LGBTQ+ protections, free markets and privatisation, and development of capital markets and free capital flows within the EU.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto strongly supports liberal political institutions like the Constitutional Tribunal, robust social rights protections against discrimination, economic liberalism through free market and competition support, and financial market reforms targeting transparency and anti-fraud measures, indicating a high overall liberalism stance.

chunk 2 · The manifesto strongly supports democratic institutions, minority protections, economic liberalization including tax simplification, and restoring financial market credibility, demonstrating a pro-liberal stance across political, social, economic, and financial dimensions.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The manifesto is strongly classical liberal, advocating for the separation of powers, free-market competition, privatization, civil rights including civil unions, and the development of capital markets.

chunk 2 · The text is highly classical liberal, strongly advocating for the rule of law, separation of church and state, free markets, privatization, and capital market development.
