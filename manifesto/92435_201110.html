WarningHow to read this page

The scores are holistic judgements. Each model read the whole manifesto text (in chunks of up to 20,000 tokens) and rated every dimension from the text as a whole. The passages quoted further down are examples the model cited, not the basis of the score: a score that rests on two paragraphs cannot be reduced to a few words, and a quoted passage alone will often look too weak, or too strong, for the number next to it. Read the models’ own explanations first; they state the reasoning behind each block of scores. The quotes then show where in the text the models looked.

Populism, populist ideology and liberalism

Thirteen dimensions scored on 0–10 by three model families from the full manifesto text, each with a verbatim evidence quote. Scores are aggregated as in Rode & Stöckl (2026): a chunk counts if the model marks its evidence as verified and the quote is found verbatim in the manifesto chunk (and is not copied from the prompt); confidence-weighted mean within model, plain mean across models.

What the models say about this manifesto

Each model’s own explanation of its scores, one per block of dimensions, written together with the scores.

Populism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto contains only faint populist traces: a brief historical reference to elites who 'seized the state and democracy' and mild criticism of those who 'sow doubt,' but the dominant register is technocratic, modernisation-oriented governance rhetoric rather than a people-vs-elite frame.

chunk 2 · The manifesto is a governing-party programme (Platforma Obywatelska) focused on technocratic achievements and pragmatic policy; it contains virtually no anti-elite rhetoric, people-as-sovereign framing, or Manichean good-vs-evil narrative, yielding an overall populism score of 1.

chunk 3 · The text is primarily a technocratic governing-record document with minimal populist framing; references to "Poles" are rhetorical but not strongly people-centric, and the only elite-criticism targets the parliamentary opposition rather than a corrupt establishment.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto expresses moderate anti-elitism and moral conflict by opposing those who 'zawłaszczyli państwo' and radical disruptors, while emphasizing a vision of a strong, modern Poland representing 'the people', reflecting moderate populist themes.

chunk 2 · The manifesto expresses moderate anti-elitism and people-centrism through emphasis on national interests and citizen focus, but lacks strong moral dichotomies, resulting in a low overall populism score.

chunk 3 · The manifesto does not contain explicit references to populist themes such as anti-elitism, people-centrism, or Manichean moral struggles, indicating no evidence of populism.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text exhibits mild populism, framing past political opponents as having hijacked the state and democracy while positioning the party as a defender of the citizens' aspirations.

chunk 2 · The text contains no populist rhetoric, anti-elitist framing, or moralized good-versus-evil divisions.

chunk 3 · The text contains no populist rhetoric, anti-elitist framing, or moralized good-versus-evil divisions.

Populist ideology

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The party explicitly rejects left-right positioning ('Nie jesteśmy ani z prawicy, ani z lewicy') and brands itself 'Nowe Centrum,' making centrist-technocratic the dominant ideological flavour; the very limited populist content carries no strong left or right programmatic charge.

chunk 2 · The text is centrist-pragmatist with no strong left or right populist ideological flavour; it invokes national interest and pragmatism ("Realizujemy nasze interesy, ale nie jesteśmy interesowni") without class-based or nativist appeals, placing it marginally in the centrist populist ideology category at a very low level.

chunk 3 · The manifesto is predominantly centrist-technocratic, emphasising fiscal responsibility, EU funds management, and market-oriented reforms, with only minor left-leaning elements (equal pay, minimum wage) and no right-populist identity or anti-immigration content.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The text explicitly states it is neither right nor left, embracing a centrist populist ideology open to all citizens, including social democrats and conservatives, emphasizing civic ideals without clear left or right dominance.

chunk 2 · The text shows a centrist populist ideology with some left-leaning social policies and right-leaning national security stances, reflecting a mixed but moderate populist ideological profile.

chunk 3 · There is no explicit evidence in the text of populist ideological stances on the left, right, or centrist populism; thus, no populist ideology is detected.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The party explicitly rejects left-right divisions, positioning itself as a centrist, catch-all platform for all citizens.

chunk 2 · There is no evidence of populist ideology as the text focuses on mainstream governance, defense, and administrative reforms.

chunk 3 · There is no evidence of populist ideology as the text focuses on mainstream technocratic governance and policy achievements.

Liberalism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto is strongly pro-liberal across all four dimensions: it champions constitutional checks and balances, media independence, gender pay equality, VAT reduction, market competition (including for health insurance), and fiscal consolidation aimed at lowering interest rates and debt-to-GDP ratios.

chunk 2 · The manifesto is strongly liberal across all four dimensions: it champions judicial independence and rule of law, anti-discrimination in healthcare, sweeping deregulation and market competition, and modernisation of financial/legal enforcement procedures, yielding a high overall liberalism score of 8.

chunk 3 · The manifesto is broadly pro-liberal: it champions tax cuts, deregulation, privatisation, and competition (economic); anti-discrimination and equal pay (social); prudential banking safeguards that enable market stability (financial); and rule-based governance with rights protections (political).

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto strongly supports liberal principles including respect for rule of law, political rights, social equality measures like equal pay, economic liberalization such as VAT reduction, and significant investment in education and research infrastructure, indicating a pro-liberal stance.

chunk 2 · The manifesto strongly supports political and social liberalism with laws protecting patient rights and social welfare, alongside economic liberalism promoting market freedom and investment, indicating a generally pro-liberal stance.

chunk 3 · The manifesto explicitly discusses liberal policies including tax reductions, support for political rights and institutions, gender equality measures, economic deregulation, and strong financial market protections, demonstrating a pro-liberal stance overall.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text strongly supports classical liberal values, emphasizing the rule of law, individual freedom, anti-discrimination measures, and the reduction of regulatory barriers.

chunk 2 · The text strongly supports classical liberal principles, emphasizing deregulation, expanding economic freedom, and promoting democratic values.

chunk 3 · The text strongly supports classical liberal and ordoliberal principles, including privatization, deregulation, non-discrimination, and prudential financial regulations that secure banking stability.
