WarningHow to read this page

The scores are holistic judgements. Each model read the whole manifesto text (in chunks of up to 20,000 tokens) and rated every dimension from the text as a whole. The passages quoted further down are examples the model cited, not the basis of the score: a score that rests on two paragraphs cannot be reduced to a few words, and a quoted passage alone will often look too weak, or too strong, for the number next to it. Read the models’ own explanations first; they state the reasoning behind each block of scores. The quotes then show where in the text the models looked.

Populism, populist ideology and liberalism

Thirteen dimensions scored on 0–10 by three model families from the full manifesto text, each with a verbatim evidence quote. Scores are aggregated as in Rode & Stöckl (2026): a chunk counts if the model marks its evidence as verified and the quote is found verbatim in the manifesto chunk (and is not copied from the prompt); confidence-weighted mean within model, plain mean across models.

What the models say about this manifesto

Each model’s own explanation of its scores, one per block of dimensions, written together with the scores.

Populism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto shows moderate populist features primarily through sharp Manichean contrasts between ODS's responsible governance and ČSSD's alleged irresponsibility and populism, but it explicitly rejects populism and frames its appeals in technocratic and programmatic terms rather than invoking a unified virtuous people against corrupt elites.

chunk 2 · The text is a mainstream centre-right party manifesto (ODS) focused on policy detail; it contains only isolated partisan attacks on ČSSD/KSČM and occasional citizen-empowerment rhetoric, but no sustained people-vs-elite framing, no Manichean moral narrative, and no populist mobilisation of "the people" as a unified virtuous bloc.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto explicitly criticizes the left-wing opposition (ČSSD and KSČM) as irresponsible elites and uses moralized language to depict a struggle, while emphasizing the people’s interests in a centrist populist manner.

chunk 2 · The manifesto explicitly criticizes the 'populism Paroubkovy ČSSD' and their negative impact, indicating a low anti-elitism and manichean populist stance, with no evidence of strong people-centrism.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text exhibits low populism, framing political opponents as acting on short-term interests and calling corruption a social evil, but it explicitly rejects populist economic solutions.

chunk 2 · The text exhibits low populism, only criticizing the opposition party's 'populism' and 'cowardice' rather than framing politics as a moral struggle between a pure people and a corrupt elite.

Populist ideology

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto's populist elements lean right-wing, emphasizing controlled immigration, anti-statism, free markets, and conservative social values, while explicitly rejecting left-wing redistribution and labeling ČSSD's social promises as irresponsible populism.

chunk 2 · The manifesto's limited populist signals lean mildly right-wing (immigration restriction, national identity, anti-communist framing) rather than left-wing redistribution or centrist people-elite appeals, but the overall populist ideological intensity is very low given the predominantly technocratic, policy-oriented content.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The text shows a right-leaning populist ideology emphasizing national economic responsibility, anti-left elite rhetoric, and centrist people-elite appeals without clear leftist redistribution programs.

chunk 2 · The text opposes uncontrolled immigration and closed communities, reflecting right-leaning populist ideology, while also emphasizing limited state competence, indicating a centrist populist flavor overall.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The populist ideology is primarily right-leaning, focusing on national identity, integration of foreigners, and strict control over immigration.

chunk 2 · The populist ideology is slightly right-leaning, focusing on controlling immigration and protecting national rules, while rejecting left-wing policies.

Liberalism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto is strongly pro-liberal across all dimensions: it champions free markets, low taxes, deregulation, privatization, fiscal rules, transparency in public finances, and rule of law reforms, while explicitly opposing state intervention in energy pricing and excessive social spending.

chunk 2 · The manifesto is strongly pro-liberal across political (rule of law, judicial reform, anti-corruption, democratic accountability), economic (deregulation, free trade, competition, privatisation), and financial-market dimensions (insurance competition, voluntary savings), while showing a mixed social-liberal stance by explicitly rejecting positive discrimination for minorities even as it upholds individual rights.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto strongly supports liberal economic policies, free markets, political rights, social protections for vulnerable groups, and prudent financial market regulation enabling market function.

chunk 2 · The manifesto strongly supports rule of law, individual rights, free markets, and financial transparency, with explicit references to democracy, human rights, market freedom, and transparent public spending.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The manifesto strongly advocates classical liberal principles, emphasizing free markets, privatization, low taxes, and judicial efficiency.

chunk 2 · The manifesto strongly supports classical liberal principles, emphasizing the rule of law, private property rights, and free-market competition, while rejecting positive discrimination.
