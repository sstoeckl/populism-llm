WarningHow to read this page

The scores are holistic judgements. Each model read the whole manifesto text (in chunks of up to 20,000 tokens) and rated every dimension from the text as a whole. The passages quoted further down are examples the model cited, not the basis of the score: a score that rests on two paragraphs cannot be reduced to a few words, and a quoted passage alone will often look too weak, or too strong, for the number next to it. Read the models’ own explanations first; they state the reasoning behind each block of scores. The quotes then show where in the text the models looked.

Populism, populist ideology and liberalism

Thirteen dimensions scored on 0–10 by three model families from the full manifesto text, each with a verbatim evidence quote. Scores are aggregated as in Rode & Stöckl (2026): a chunk counts if the model marks its evidence as verified and the quote is found verbatim in the manifesto chunk (and is not copied from the prompt); confidence-weighted mean within model, plain mean across models.

What the models say about this manifesto

Each model’s own explanation of its scores, one per block of dimensions, written together with the scores.

Populism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto is a detailed policy platform with minimal populist rhetoric; it shows mild concern about government overreach and individual rights, and occasional criticism of the Labour Government's scheme, but does not frame politics as a Manichean struggle between virtuous people and corrupt elites.

chunk 2 · The manifesto contains only mild populist elements: it criticises the Labour government's economic mismanagement but does so in technocratic rather than moralistic terms, with no sustained people-vs-elite framing or Manichean narrative.

chunk 3 · The manifesto shows minimal populist characteristics: it occasionally invokes "local people" and farmer confidence, but frames politics as cooperative partnership between government, enterprise, and communities rather than as a struggle between virtuous people and corrupt elites.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto expresses moderate anti-elitism and people-centrism through emphasis on farmer confidence and national unity, but lacks strong moral dichotomies.

chunk 2 · The manifesto contains no explicit references to populist themes such as anti-elitism, people-centrism, or moralized good-vs-evil struggles, indicating no evidence of populism.

chunk 3 · The manifesto contains no explicit discussion or rhetoric indicative of populism or its sub-dimensions.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text contains very minimal populist rhetoric, with only a minor anti-statist warning against the arbitrary interference of the government.

chunk 2 · There is no evidence of populist rhetoric, anti-elitism, or manichean framing in the provided text.

chunk 3 · There is no evidence of populist rhetoric, anti-elitism, or moralized good-versus-evil framing in this policy document.

Populist ideology

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto leans mildly right-populist through its emphasis on controlled immigration and national self-reliance, but the overall ideological tone is mainstream centre-right conservatism rather than strongly populist in any direction.

chunk 2 · The manifesto is primarily a centre-right policy document emphasising free enterprise, fiscal restraint, and limited immigration; its populist ideological content is weak and does not clearly align with left or right populism.

chunk 3 · The manifesto's weak populist signals lean slightly right (land ownership restrictions on foreigners, private enterprise emphasis) but are primarily technocratic and centrist, with no strong left or right populist programmatic thrust.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The text shows a centrist populist ideology with controlled immigration and support for workers' rights, balancing left and right populist elements.

chunk 2 · There is no explicit evidence of populist ideological stances, left, right, or centrist, in the manifesto text.

chunk 3 · There is no evidence of populist ideological framing on the left, right, or centrist populism in the manifesto text.

Gemini

chunk 1 · There is no clear populist ideological framing present in the text, as the focus is on mainstream conservative-liberal policy proposals.

chunk 2 · The text does not contain populist ideological framing, focusing instead on standard programmatic policy proposals.

chunk 3 · The text does not contain populist ideological framing, focusing instead on standard programmatic policy proposals.

Liberalism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto is broadly liberal in its emphasis on individual rights, rule of law, anti-discrimination, and preference for private enterprise over state intervention, though it also supports protective tariffs, import licensing, and state-directed housing finance, producing a mixed economic-liberal picture.

chunk 2 · The manifesto consistently promotes free enterprise, democratic governance, individual rights, and market-oriented economic policies, while also supporting some state intervention in health and welfare, yielding a moderately pro-liberal overall profile.

chunk 3 · The manifesto is broadly pro-liberal across all four dimensions: it upholds rule of law and independent courts, promotes anti-discrimination and women's equality, champions private enterprise and trade openness, and supports investor/creditor protection in financial markets.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto strongly supports political rights, social protections, and economic liberalism through support for small business and individual rights, though financial market specifics are absent.

chunk 2 · The manifesto explicitly supports democratic consultation, equality of educational opportunities, and a dynamic free enterprise economic system, reflecting a pro-liberal stance overall.

chunk 3 · The manifesto explicitly supports rule of law, equality and anti-discrimination policies, economic development through free enterprise, and social equality measures, indicating a pro-liberal stance overall.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The manifesto strongly supports classical liberal principles, emphasizing individual rights, anti-discrimination, private enterprise, and market-enabling financial schemes.

chunk 2 · The manifesto strongly supports classical liberal principles, emphasizing a free enterprise system, democratic local governance, non-discrimination, and a more flexible interest rate policy.

chunk 3 · The manifesto strongly supports classical liberal principles, including independent courts, gender equality, private enterprise, and investor protection.
