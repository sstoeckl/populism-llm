WarningHow to read this page

The scores are holistic judgements. Each model read the whole manifesto text (in chunks of up to 20,000 tokens) and rated every dimension from the text as a whole. The passages quoted further down are examples the model cited, not the basis of the score: a score that rests on two paragraphs cannot be reduced to a few words, and a quoted passage alone will often look too weak, or too strong, for the number next to it. Read the models’ own explanations first; they state the reasoning behind each block of scores. The quotes then show where in the text the models looked.

Populism, populist ideology and liberalism

Thirteen dimensions scored on 0–10 by three model families from the full manifesto text, each with a verbatim evidence quote. Scores are aggregated as in Rode & Stöckl (2026): a chunk counts if the model marks its evidence as verified and the quote is found verbatim in the manifesto chunk (and is not copied from the prompt); confidence-weighted mean within model, plain mean across models.

What the models say about this manifesto

Each model’s own explanation of its scores, one per block of dimensions, written together with the scores.

Populism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto consistently contrasts the virtuous, citizen-centred PDSR against the corrupt and incompetent ruling coalition, invoking the people as the true decision-makers and framing the incumbent government's record as a moral and economic catastrophe, yielding moderate populist scores across all three sub-dimensions.

chunk 2 · The manifesto shows mild populist tendencies: it criticises the incumbent coalition's governance failures and the State Property Fund's mismanagement as harmful to citizens, and frames poverty as a fundamental national problem affecting ordinary people, but it does not construct a strong Manichean people-vs-elite narrative and is predominantly a technocratic policy programme.

chunk 3 · The text shows minimal populism: it criticizes political clientelism in administration and frames reforms as serving citizens, but does not construct a strong people-vs-elite narrative or moralized Manichean struggle.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto emphasizes participatory democracy centered on the citizen, critiques the current elite government, and stresses social solidarity, reflecting moderate populist themes with some anti-elitism and moral critique.

chunk 2 · The manifesto explicitly criticizes elite mismanagement causing harm to the state and citizens, centers the citizen in politics, and highlights combating corruption, indicating moderate populist anti-elitism, people-centrism, and moral struggle.

chunk 3 · The manifesto does not contain explicit references to anti-elitism, people-centrism, or Manichean moral struggles, thus no evidence of populism is found.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text strongly frames the current ruling coalition as an out-of-touch elite that treats citizens as mere electoral maneuver, contrasting them with the virtuous, participatory 'people'.

chunk 2 · The text exhibits moderate populism by criticizing the previous administration's privatization damages to citizens and emphasizing the restoration of parliament as the supreme representative of the people.

chunk 3 · The text exhibits minimal populism, focusing primarily on administrative reforms and anti-clientelism rather than moralized people-vs-elite struggles.

Populist ideology

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The party explicitly identifies as centre-left social-democratic, emphasising redistribution, solidarity, equality of opportunity, and state-led social protection, placing it firmly in left-populist territory with only marginal nationalist/right-populist elements around sovereignty and national identity.

chunk 2 · The manifesto leans mildly left-populist through its emphasis on raising the minimum wage, expanding social protection, combating poverty, and redistributive social policies, while also invoking national identity and cultural heritage; the overall ideological flavour is social-democratic rather than strongly populist.

chunk 3 · The text blends mild left-leaning social protection commitments with right-leaning national/cultural identity references (Romanian Orthodox Church, national consciousness), without a dominant populist ideological flavor.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The text strongly supports left-leaning social democratic policies like combating poverty and state authority restoration, while endorsing European integration and security, showing a centrist-left populist ideology.

chunk 2 · The text combines left-leaning anti-corruption and social justice themes with right-leaning anti-extremism and minority protection, alongside centrist citizen-focused governance, reflecting a mixed populist ideological stance.

chunk 3 · There is no explicit discussion of left, right, or centrist populist ideological themes in the text, indicating no populist ideological stance.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The populist ideology is heavily left-leaning, emphasizing social-democratic values, social justice, solidarity, and state-led redistribution to combat poverty.

chunk 2 · The populist ideology is predominantly left-wing, focusing heavily on state-led poverty reduction, social protection, and welfare programs for vulnerable groups.

chunk 3 · The populist elements are limited to centrist administrative anti-clientelism without strong left-wing or right-wing populist framing.

Liberalism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto endorses democratic institutions, rule of law, private property, market competition, and EU integration (pro-liberal), but also advocates significant state intervention in the economy, selective industrial policy, subsidies, and tighter financial supervision, producing mixed scores on economic and financial-market liberalism.

chunk 2 · The manifesto consistently endorses democratic rule of law, judicial independence, gender equality, free-market competition with private capital at the centre, and prudential protection of financial-market participants, placing it firmly in a pro-liberal, social-democratic tradition with moderate state intervention to correct market failures.

chunk 3 · The text is moderately liberal: it explicitly endorses democratic principles, human rights, minority protections, religious pluralism, and private initiative, while also advocating state social programs and public administration reform aligned with EU standards.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto advocates for rule of law, social rights, market economy with regulation, and financial system modernization aligned with European standards, indicating a pro-liberal stance overall.

chunk 2 · The manifesto strongly supports democratic institutions, minority rights, market-based economic growth, and financial market transparency, indicating a pro-liberal stance across political, social, economic, and financial dimensions.

chunk 3 · The manifesto explicitly supports democracy, human rights, minority protections, cultural pluralism, and economic revitalization through agriculture and local development, reflecting a moderate pro-liberal stance.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text supports classical liberal institutions like the rule of law, private property, and European integration, but balances them with strong social-democratic state intervention and welfare guarantees.

chunk 2 · The text strongly supports classical liberal principles including the rule of law, minority rights, market competition, and capital market development, balanced by state regulation to correct market failures.

chunk 3 · The text strongly supports liberal principles, including minority rights, religious pluralism, private initiative, and structured credit access.
