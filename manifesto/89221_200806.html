WarningHow to read this page

The scores are holistic judgements. Each model read the whole manifesto text (in chunks of up to 20,000 tokens) and rated every dimension from the text as a whole. The passages quoted further down are examples the model cited, not the basis of the score: a score that rests on two paragraphs cannot be reduced to a few words, and a quoted passage alone will often look too weak, or too strong, for the number next to it. Read the models’ own explanations first; they state the reasoning behind each block of scores. The quotes then show where in the text the models looked.

Populism, populist ideology and liberalism

Thirteen dimensions scored on 0–10 by three model families from the full manifesto text, each with a verbatim evidence quote. Scores are aggregated as in Rode & Stöckl (2026): a chunk counts if the model marks its evidence as verified and the quote is found verbatim in the manifesto chunk (and is not copied from the prompt); confidence-weighted mean within model, plain mean across models.

What the models say about this manifesto

Each model’s own explanation of its scores, one per block of dimensions, written together with the scores.

Populism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto contains moderate populist elements: it criticises the incumbent VMRO-DPMNE government as self-serving and deliberately harmful, frames elections as a moral choice between integration and isolation, and appeals to the ordinary citizen's welfare, but these are embedded in a programmatic social-democratic platform rather than a full people-vs-elite populist narrative.

chunk 2 · The text contains moderate populist elements: the ruling party (VMRO-DPMNE) is criticised for partisanship, corruption and destroying public institutions, framing a good-vs-bad political struggle, but the overall tone is technocratic and policy-focused rather than strongly people-vs-elite populist.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto explicitly criticizes the ruling elite's control over institutions and media, emphasizes the unity and virtue of the Macedonian people, and frames political choice as a struggle between integration and isolation, reflecting moderate populist themes.

chunk 2 · The manifesto does not provide explicit content related to populism dimensions such as anti-elitism, people-centrism, or manichean narratives, thus no evidence of populism is found.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text exhibits moderate populism by criticizing the ruling elite for seeking absolute power and controlling the media, while framing the election as a crucial choice for the people.

chunk 2 · The text criticizes the ruling class for using culture as a ritualistic mirror to flatter itself, showing moderate anti-elitism, but lacks strong people-centrism or moralized manichean framing.

Populist ideology

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto is primarily social-democratic left-leaning, advocating progressive taxation, workers' rights, social equality, and redistribution, with a secondary emphasis on national identity preservation; right-wing populist or centrist-populist elements are minimal.

chunk 2 · The manifesto is clearly left-leaning social-democratic, emphasising solidarity, equality, universal healthcare, and state support for youth and culture, with only minor references to national sovereignty that do not constitute right-wing populism.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The text supports left-leaning populist ideology by advocating for economic justice and support for vulnerable citizens, while also emphasizing national identity protection and centrist political partnership, showing a mixed but overall moderate populist ideological stance.

chunk 2 · There is no explicit discussion or evidence of populist ideological stances (left, right, or centrist) in the manifesto text provided.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The populist ideology is predominantly left-wing, focusing on social justice, solidarity, and workers' rights, combined with a moderate defense of national identity.

chunk 2 · The populist elements are framed within a social-democratic ideology that emphasizes equality, solidarity, and public services, with no right-wing populist elements.

Liberalism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto is broadly pro-liberal: it strongly defends media independence, democratic institutions, rule of law, gender equality, and market-oriented economic reforms including capital gains tax abolition and capital market stimulation, while also proposing progressive taxation and social protections that temper pure economic liberalism.

chunk 2 · The manifesto strongly supports judicial independence, rule of law, human rights, and anti-corruption measures (pro-liberal politically and socially), while economic liberalism is present only modestly through tax incentives for companies; financial market policy is entirely absent.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto strongly supports liberal principles including respect for democratic institutions, promotion of social equality and minority rights, coordinated economic policies for growth, and maintaining stable financial markets, indicating a pro-liberal stance overall.

chunk 2 · The manifesto explicitly supports liberal principles such as independent judiciary, support for youth organizations promoting social inclusion, preventive health programs, and energy efficiency, indicating a pro-liberal stance in political, social, and economic dimensions.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The manifesto strongly supports classical liberal principles, including parliamentary democracy, media freedom, equal opportunities, and a strong market economy.

chunk 2 · The text strongly supports classical liberal principles including the rule of law, judicial independence, human rights, and tax incentives for private enterprise.
