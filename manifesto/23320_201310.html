WarningHow to read this page

The scores are holistic judgements. Each model read the whole manifesto text (in chunks of up to 20,000 tokens) and rated every dimension from the text as a whole. The passages quoted further down are examples the model cited, not the basis of the score: a score that rests on two paragraphs cannot be reduced to a few words, and a quoted passage alone will often look too weak, or too strong, for the number next to it. Read the models’ own explanations first; they state the reasoning behind each block of scores. The quotes then show where in the text the models looked.

Populism, populist ideology and liberalism

Thirteen dimensions scored on 0–10 by three model families from the full manifesto text, each with a verbatim evidence quote. Scores are aggregated as in Rode & Stöckl (2026): a chunk counts if the model marks its evidence as verified and the quote is found verbatim in the manifesto chunk (and is not copied from the prompt); confidence-weighted mean within model, plain mean across models.

What the models say about this manifesto

Each model’s own explanation of its scores, one per block of dimensions, written together with the scores.

Populism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto shows mild populist elements: it criticises the incumbent CSV party for governance failures and frames citizens as the central concern, but it does not construct a strong people-vs-elite dichotomy or moralistic good-vs-evil narrative; the tone is predominantly programmatic and technocratic.

chunk 2 · The manifesto is a detailed social-democratic policy programme with only mild populist undertones: it occasionally contrasts 'the people' with conservative/neoliberal forces and criticises profit-maximisation, but it does not frame politics as a Manichean struggle between a virtuous people and a corrupt elite, and it relies overwhelmingly on technocratic, institutional language.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · Das Manifest kritisiert etablierte Parteien als reformunwillig, betont die zentrale Rolle der Bürger und verweist auf Vertrauensverlust in Institutionen, was eine moderate bis starke populistische Haltung zeigt.

chunk 2 · The manifesto explicitly opposes worsening working conditions and emphasizes solidarity and responsibility for society, indicating low populist anti-elitism and moderate people-centrism but lacks strong moral dichotomies.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text exhibits moderate populism by contrasting the 'Bürgerinnen und Bürger' with the long-ruling CSV-minister establishment, though it remains a mainstream social-democratic program.

chunk 2 · The text contains no populist rhetoric, anti-elitist framing, or moralized good-versus-evil divisions between the pure people and corrupt elites.

Populist ideology

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto is clearly left-leaning: it advocates a wealth tax, redistribution, strong welfare state, workers' rights, and social solidarity, while the right-wing element (preference for domestic workers) is minor and not framed in nativist terms.

chunk 2 · The manifesto is clearly left-leaning social-democratic: it champions workers' rights, redistribution, social solidarity, and stronger regulation of markets, with no right-wing populist or nativist content and only minimal generic people-centred appeals.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · Die LSAP betont sozialistische Reformen, soziale Gerechtigkeit und Bürgerbeteiligung, zeigt moderate nationalistische Tendenzen durch Kirchen-Staat-Trennung, insgesamt eine linke bis zentristische populistische Ideologie.

chunk 2 · The text strongly supports social justice and societal progress, with moderate centrist appeals to solidarity and some right-leaning labor protections, reflecting a left-leaning populist ideology overall.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The populist elements are clearly left-wing, focusing on wealth redistribution, a 'Reichensteuer', and protecting the welfare state against corporate/capital interests.

chunk 2 · There is no evidence of populist ideology as the text represents a standard social-democratic program without populist framing.

Liberalism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto is strongly pro-liberal on political and social dimensions (rule of law, separation of powers, same-sex marriage, church-state separation), mixed on economic liberalism (combining market-friendly measures with redistribution and state intervention), and mixed on financial markets (supporting a clean, well-regulated financial centre while calling for tighter oversight and curbing speculation).

chunk 2 · The manifesto scores high on political liberalism (press freedom, rule of law, democratic participation) and social liberalism (anti-discrimination, gender equality, LGBTQ+ rights including same-sex marriage), but is mixed on economic liberalism (supporting labour market regulation and social protection while keeping some market incentives), and is moderately anti-liberal on financial markets (calling for taming financial markets and introducing a financial transaction tax).

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · Das Manifest befürwortet demokratische Grundrechte, soziale Gleichstellung, Wettbewerbsschutz und eine transparente Finanzmarktaufsicht, was eine überwiegend liberale Position in politischen, sozialen, wirtschaftlichen und Finanzfragen belegt.

chunk 2 · The manifesto strongly supports political rights, anti-discrimination, social equality, economic growth with social cohesion, and calls for market regulation, indicating a pro-liberal stance overall.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text strongly supports political and social liberalism through constitutional reforms, separation of church and state, and LGBTQ+ rights, while advocating a regulated social-market economy with tighter financial oversight.

chunk 2 · The text strongly supports political and social liberalism (democracy, rule of law, gender equality, LGBTQ+ rights) but advocates for a highly regulated social market economy, rejecting neoliberalism and financial speculation.
