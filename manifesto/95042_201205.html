WarningHow to read this page

The scores are holistic judgements. Each model read the whole manifesto text (in chunks of up to 20,000 tokens) and rated every dimension from the text as a whole. The passages quoted further down are examples the model cited, not the basis of the score: a score that rests on two paragraphs cannot be reduced to a few words, and a quoted passage alone will often look too weak, or too strong, for the number next to it. Read the models’ own explanations first; they state the reasoning behind each block of scores. The quotes then show where in the text the models looked.

Populism, populist ideology and liberalism

Thirteen dimensions scored on 0–10 by three model families from the full manifesto text, each with a verbatim evidence quote. Scores are aggregated as in Rode & Stöckl (2026): a chunk counts if the model marks its evidence as verified and the quote is found verbatim in the manifesto chunk (and is not copied from the prompt); confidence-weighted mean within model, plain mean across models.

What the models say about this manifesto

Each model’s own explanation of its scores, one per block of dimensions, written together with the scores.

Populism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto (PREOKRET/LDP, Serbia 2012) shows moderate populist features: it blames "generations of unsuccessful politicians" and corrupt elites for Serbia's decline, appeals to citizens as the legitimate political subject, and contrasts itself with "populists," but it consistently grounds its critique in institutional and policy analysis rather than a pure good-people vs. evil-elite narrative.

chunk 2 · The text criticizes the corrupt political establishment and ethno-nationalist elites who have ruined Serbian society, but frames this primarily as a programmatic liberal-democratic reform agenda rather than a strong populist appeal to 'the people' against elites.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto emphasizes a new political morality, direct engagement with the people, and contrasts its approach with populist promises, showing moderate anti-elitism, people-centrism, and some moral struggle rhetoric.

chunk 2 · The manifesto criticizes a corrupt elite and political struggle for power, invokes a cultural identity of the people, and highlights moral decay and nationalistic hatred, indicating low to moderate populist elements.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text exhibits moderate populism by criticizing the corrupt and unsuccessful political class while positioning itself as the voice of citizens tired of political lies.

chunk 2 · The text criticizes the corrupt political establishment and party nomenclatures that control cultural and social institutions, framing them as self-serving elites.

Populist ideology

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto's populist flavor is predominantly centrist-technocratic (pro-EU, pro-NATO, pro-reform, anti-monopoly) with only marginal left (social protection for the vulnerable) and right (cultural heritage, regional identity) elements; it explicitly distances itself from populism and left-wing statism alike.

chunk 2 · The text is associated with the Liberal Democratic Party (LDP) and promotes free markets, European integration, media freedom, and minority rights, placing it firmly in a liberal-centrist ideological space with only marginal populist left or right elements.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The text supports left-leaning economic justice and redistribution, centrist people-elite appeals, and a moderate pro-NATO stance reflecting right-leaning security concerns, indicating a mixed but overall moderate populist ideology.

chunk 2 · The text reflects left populist critique of corruption, right populist nationalistic references, and centrist populist appeals to European values, showing a mixed populist ideological stance.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The populist elements are centrist, focusing on anti-establishment reform and modernization without adopting left-wing anti-corporate or right-wing nativist ideologies.

chunk 2 · The populist critique is centrist, focusing on anti-corruption, transparency, and efficiency without adopting left-wing anti-capitalist or right-wing nativist ideologies.

Liberalism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto is strongly pro-liberal across all four dimensions: it champions open market economy, privatization, competition, minority and human rights, constitutional reform for rule of law and judicial independence, and open capital markets with transparent investment, consistently framing state intervention as an obstacle to be reduced.

chunk 2 · The manifesto strongly endorses free markets in culture and healthcare (privatization, deregulation, competition), media freedom, minority and individual rights, and European legal standards, reflecting a consistently pro-liberal stance across political, social, and economic dimensions.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto strongly advocates rule of law, minority rights, market economy, and transparent financial and tax systems, reflecting a clear pro-liberal stance across political, social, economic, and financial dimensions.

chunk 2 · The manifesto supports media freedom, human rights, and a free cultural market, but lacks discussion on financial market liberalism, indicating moderate classical liberalism.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The manifesto is strongly classical liberal, advocating for the rule of law, individual liberties, minority rights, and a free-market economy free from state and party monopolies.

chunk 2 · The text strongly advocates for classical liberal principles, including free markets, media freedom, human rights, and the integration of private enterprise into public services.
