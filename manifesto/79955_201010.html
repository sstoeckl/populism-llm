WarningHow to read this page

The scores are holistic judgements. Each model read the whole manifesto text (in chunks of up to 20,000 tokens) and rated every dimension from the text as a whole. The passages quoted further down are examples the model cited, not the basis of the score: a score that rests on two paragraphs cannot be reduced to a few words, and a quoted passage alone will often look too weak, or too strong, for the number next to it. Read the models’ own explanations first; they state the reasoning behind each block of scores. The quotes then show where in the text the models looked.

Populism, populist ideology and liberalism

Thirteen dimensions scored on 0–10 by three model families from the full manifesto text, each with a verbatim evidence quote. Scores are aggregated as in Rode & Stöckl (2026): a chunk counts if the model marks its evidence as verified and the quote is found verbatim in the manifesto chunk (and is not copied from the prompt); confidence-weighted mean within model, plain mean across models.

What the models say about this manifesto

Each model’s own explanation of its scores, one per block of dimensions, written together with the scores.

Populism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto invokes the Serbian people as a unified virtuous group whose interests must always come first, frames certain post-war elites and international actors as hostile to Republika Srpska, but stops short of a fully Manichean good-vs-evil narrative, yielding a moderate overall populism score.

chunk 2 · The text invokes the will and interests of citizens as the ultimate political reference point and frames corruption/crime as the primary enemy of society, but stops short of a full populist good-people vs. evil-elite dichotomy, instead channelling grievances into institutional reform proposals.

chunk 3 · The manifesto shows moderate populist features: it criticises the ruling establishment for devastating governance, invokes the will of the people through referenda, and frames political opponents as dangerous and malicious actors, but these elements are embedded in a broader programmatic text rather than constituting a thoroughgoing populist discourse.

chunk 4 · The text consistently invokes the will of the people ("ВОЉА НАРОДА") as the supreme political guide, frames the current ruling establishment as having caused catastrophic devastation, and positions the party as the defender of the people against corrupt elites and illegitimate international oversight.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto explicitly frames elites as adversaries through references to international pressures and political punishments targeting party cadres, emphasizes the party as a representative of the broad 'people' including the middle class, and portrays a historical struggle of the Serbian people against oppression, indicating a moralized conflict narrative.

chunk 2 · The manifesto does not contain explicit references to populist themes such as anti-elitism, people-centrism, or Manichean moral struggles, thus no evidence of populism is found.

chunk 3 · The manifesto expresses moderate anti-elitism through support for social security systems, emphasizes people-centrism by highlighting national unity, and shows a balanced moral struggle against wrongdoing, reflecting moderate populism overall.

chunk 4 · The manifesto explicitly criticizes the international elite's imposition and sanctions, emphasizing the 'will of the people' as central, reflecting anti-elitism and people-centrism with some moral conflict framing.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text exhibits moderate populism by framing the party as the authentic voice of the unified Serbian people against corrupt, anti-national elites and hostile external forces.

chunk 2 · The text exhibits moderate populism by emphasizing the will of the majority of citizens and criticizing financial centers of power that influence the media.

chunk 3 · The text exhibits moderate populism by framing the ruling establishment as destructive and corrupt, while emphasizing the need to respect the direct will and voice of the people.

chunk 4 · The text exhibits strong populism by framing the ruling establishment as corrupt and destructive while declaring the will of the people as its ultimate guide.

Populist ideology

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The dominant ideological flavor is right-wing populism centered on Serbian national identity, territorial preservation of Republika Srpska, and resistance to external (international) interference, with only minor left-leaning social-solidarity elements drawn from Orthodox tradition rather than class politics.

chunk 2 · The manifesto blends moderate left-leaning redistribution and social-state commitments with right-leaning domestic-market protectionism and national economic sovereignty, without a dominant populist ideological pole.

chunk 3 · The populist ideology is predominantly right-leaning, centred on Serbian national identity, protection of Republika Srpska's autonomy, ethnic-national interests, and scepticism toward NATO and Western-led international integration, with only secondary social-redistributive elements.

chunk 4 · The populist ideology is predominantly right-wing, centred on Serbian national identity, protection of Republika Srpska's sovereignty, resistance to international (OHR) oversight, and defence of national interests, with only marginal social-solidarity elements.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto identifies the party as a democratic center party representing the middle class majority, aligns with right-center European parties on national and economic issues, and embraces social solidarity rooted in Orthodox tradition rather than leftist ideology, reflecting a centrist to right-leaning populist ideology.

chunk 2 · There is no explicit evidence of populist ideological stances (left, right, or centrist) in the manifesto text provided.

chunk 3 · The text supports left-leaning social market economy and redistribution, right-leaning national interest protection, and centrist appeals to unity and equal rights, indicating a mixed populist ideology with moderate overall score.

chunk 4 · The text combines protection of national interests and autonomy (right-leaning), economic protectionism (left-leaning), and appeals to all citizens' interests (centrist), showing a mixed populist ideological stance.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The populist ideology is strongly right-wing, focusing heavily on national preservation, cultural identity, and the sovereignty of the Serbian people.

chunk 2 · The populist ideology is framed through a right-wing national lens, explicitly identifying the party as a national party focused on the interests of Republika Srpska.

chunk 3 · The populist ideology is strongly right-wing, focusing heavily on protecting Serbian national interests, identity, and autonomy against external and internal threats.

chunk 4 · The populist ideology is strongly right-wing, focusing on the protection of Serbian national interests and the autonomy of Republika Srpska.

Liberalism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto endorses parliamentary democracy, rule of law, separation of powers, and free-market enterprise, but tempers economic liberalism with social solidarity, state ownership of infrastructure, and a preference for domestic capital, producing a moderately pro-liberal overall profile.

chunk 2 · The text strongly endorses rule of law, separation of powers, and judicial independence (pro-liberal politically), rejects discrimination (moderately pro-liberal socially), but explicitly calls for abandoning the neoliberal model and advocates state intervention, protectionism, and a state-majority bank (mixed/anti-liberal economically and financially).

chunk 3 · The manifesto endorses a social-market economy with a leading role for private initiative and markets, supports parliamentary oversight and anti-discrimination, but also calls for broad income redistribution, state intervention in labour markets, and a strong social security system, producing a mixed liberal profile across all dimensions.

chunk 4 · The text invokes democratic order, rule of law, and human rights rhetorically, but simultaneously advocates protectionist economic policy, resistance to international oversight, and ethnic-national prioritisation, producing a mixed liberal profile across political, social, and economic dimensions.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto strongly supports democratic institutions, rule of law, and political rights; advocates cultural minority affirmation and social justice; promotes market-oriented economic policies with social solidarity; and calls for government guarantees in labor relations, indicating a moderate pro-liberal stance overall.

chunk 2 · The manifesto strongly emphasizes constitutional principles of separation of powers and rule of law, anti-discrimination, and political rights, indicating a pro-liberal political and social stance; economic and financial market reforms are discussed with moderate liberal support but also concerns, resulting in an overall moderate to high liberalism score.

chunk 3 · The manifesto endorses rule of law and judicial trust, civil rights protections, social welfare policies, and economic support for social entrepreneurship, but lacks explicit financial market policy, resulting in a moderate liberalism score.

chunk 4 · The manifesto supports democratic processes and freedoms but also emphasizes social protection and economic intervention, showing limited classical liberalism; financial market issues are not addressed.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text supports classical liberal principles such as the rule of law, separation of powers, and private enterprise, though balanced with conservative traditions and social solidarity.

chunk 2 · The text strongly supports political liberalism through the separation of powers and rule of law, but rejects economic liberalism by advocating state intervention and opposing neoliberal market policies.

chunk 3 · The text supports a social-market economy where private initiative plays a leading role alongside state-supported social security, while endorsing democratic rights and non-discrimination.

chunk 4 · The text supports democratic and legal state models but leans protectionist on economic matters by advocating for the protection of domestic production.
