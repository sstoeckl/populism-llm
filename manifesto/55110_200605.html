WarningHow to read this page

The scores are holistic judgements. Each model read the whole manifesto text (in chunks of up to 20,000 tokens) and rated every dimension from the text as a whole. The passages quoted further down are examples the model cited, not the basis of the score: a score that rests on two paragraphs cannot be reduced to a few words, and a quoted passage alone will often look too weak, or too strong, for the number next to it. Read the models’ own explanations first; they state the reasoning behind each block of scores. The quotes then show where in the text the models looked.

Populism, populist ideology and liberalism

Thirteen dimensions scored on 0–10 by three model families from the full manifesto text, each with a verbatim evidence quote. Scores are aggregated as in Rode & Stöckl (2026): a chunk counts if the model marks its evidence as verified and the quote is found verbatim in the manifesto chunk (and is not copied from the prompt); confidence-weighted mean within model, plain mean across models.

What the models say about this manifesto

Each model’s own explanation of its scores, one per block of dimensions, written together with the scores.

Populism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto shows moderate populism: it frames the ruling development model as producing corruption and abuse of power by those in charge ("κρατούντων"), invokes citizens as the virtuous collective fighting for a better tomorrow, and uses a mild good-vs-evil framing (victim vs. aggressor) in the Cyprus problem context, but the overall tone is programmatic and ecological rather than strongly populist.

chunk 2 · The text contains scattered mild anti-elite rhetoric (criticising government waste, scandals, and populist project design) but is primarily a detailed environmental/ecological policy manifesto with no sustained people-vs-elite framing or Manichean narrative.

chunk 3 · The text mildly criticises government technocrats for neglecting culture and frames privatisation as "alienation of state wealth," but lacks sustained people-vs-elite rhetoric or moral Manichean framing, yielding only low-moderate populism scores.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto explicitly criticizes elites for corruption and abuse of power, invokes the people as those suffering and seeking change, and frames the Cyprus conflict in a moral struggle against Turkish expansionism, supporting a moderate populism score.

chunk 2 · The text does not provide explicit or implicit references to populist themes such as anti-elitism, people-centrism, or moral dichotomies, thus no evidence of populism is found.

chunk 3 · The text contains no explicit references to populist themes such as anti-elitism, people-centrism, or moralized good-vs-evil struggles.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text exhibits strong populist elements by contrasting the corrupt political establishment and their "developmental" model with the virtuous "civil society" of citizens.

chunk 2 · The text criticizes political decisions based on populism and showmanship rather than functionality, showing a mild anti-populist stance.

chunk 3 · The text exhibits mild anti-elitism by criticizing government technocrats for neglecting culture and exploiting artists.

Populist ideology

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The populist flavor leans mildly left: the manifesto criticizes unbridled consumerism, labor exploitation, and profit-maximization models, and calls for social justice and redistribution of resources, while anti-globalization rhetoric is present but not dominant; right-wing identity populism is essentially absent.

chunk 2 · The manifesto leans mildly left-ecological, calling for government intervention for social solidarity and equitable development, while also invoking local identity and resources; it explicitly rejects dogmatism, placing it closer to green-centrist than strongly left or right populist.

chunk 3 · The manifesto blends mild left-leaning calls for government economic intervention and social solidarity with centrist anti-dogmatism and minor conservative-cultural references to Orthodoxy, producing a weak and mixed ideological populist profile.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The text combines left-wing themes of anti-corruption and social justice, right-wing concerns about Turkish occupation and national security, and centrist appeals to unity beyond traditional party lines, justifying a moderate populist ideology score.

chunk 2 · No explicit or implicit references to populist ideological stances (left, right, or centrist) are present in the text, indicating absence of populist ideology.

chunk 3 · There is no evidence of populist ideological content on the left, right, or centrist dimensions in the text.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The populist ideology is strongly left-wing, focusing on anti-corporate sentiment, environmental protection, and opposition to profit maximization at the expense of workers and nature.

chunk 2 · There is no clear populist ideological framing present in the text.

chunk 3 · The populist elements are centrist, focusing on cultural preservation and criticizing government technocrats without a strong left-right populist framing.

Liberalism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto is moderately liberal: it strongly endorses political rights, democratic institutions, direct democracy, and minority/civil rights protections, while taking a mixed stance on economic liberalism (protecting small businesses from competition, state health system, restricting private medical funds), and financial market policy is entirely absent from the text.

chunk 2 · The text supports transparency, public participation, and rule of law in governance, includes limited social inclusion references (disabled persons), and discusses partial privatisation of semi-state enterprises alongside government regulation, yielding a mixed but moderate liberal profile; financial markets are not discussed.

chunk 3 · The text supports strengthening and fully independent capital-market oversight, transparency for investors, and partial privatisation of the stock exchange, while simultaneously opposing full privatisation of state enterprises and favouring government economic intervention, yielding a mixed but moderately pro-liberal overall score.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto strongly supports democratic institutions, human and minority rights, social equality, and economic fairness, but lacks explicit discussion of financial market policies, resulting in a high overall liberalism score with no evidence for financial market dimension.

chunk 2 · The manifesto explicitly supports liberal principles such as strict rule of law (urban planning law), protection of civil rights (environmental protections), and economic development balanced with social and environmental concerns, but lacks discussion on financial market liberalism.

chunk 3 · The manifesto discusses government intervention for social solidarity and economic management, calls for strengthening and independence of the Capital Market Commission, and environmental impact assessments, indicating moderate pro-liberal economic and political stances.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text strongly supports political and social liberalism through human rights and anti-discrimination, but rejects economic liberalism in favor of state regulation and anti-capitalist environmentalism.

chunk 2 · The text supports transparency and minority accessibility but advocates for state control over key economic sectors and semi-governmental organizations.

chunk 3 · The text shows mixed economic views opposing full privatization of state enterprises, but strongly supports liberal financial market principles like stock exchange privatization and independent regulatory oversight.
