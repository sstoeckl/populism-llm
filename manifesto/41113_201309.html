WarningHow to read this page

The scores are holistic judgements. Each model read the whole manifesto text (in chunks of up to 20,000 tokens) and rated every dimension from the text as a whole. The passages quoted further down are examples the model cited, not the basis of the score: a score that rests on two paragraphs cannot be reduced to a few words, and a quoted passage alone will often look too weak, or too strong, for the number next to it. Read the models’ own explanations first; they state the reasoning behind each block of scores. The quotes then show where in the text the models looked.

chunk 7 · The text shows only mild populist features: occasional criticism of opaque decision-making by officials and speculation by investors, and some people-centric framing around inclusive participation, but no sustained elite-vs-people narrative, no conspiracy framing, and no Manichean moral struggle.

chunk 8 · The text contains mild criticism of the incumbent Schwarz-Gelb government as "skandalös" and "verantwortungslos" for arms exports to authoritarian states, but this is targeted partisan critique rather than a systemic anti-elite populist frame; there is no invocation of a virtuous people against a corrupt elite, and no Manichean good-vs-evil narrative beyond standard oppositional rhetoric.

chunk 1 · The manifesto is strongly pro-liberal on political and social dimensions (democracy, civil rights, anti-discrimination, LGBTQ+ equality), but mixed on economic and financial-market liberalism, combining support for market mechanisms with calls for strong regulation, wealth taxes, and financial-market controls.

chunk 8 · The text strongly endorses political liberalism (rule of law, democracy, human rights, parliamentary oversight) and social liberalism (LGBTQ+ rights, women's rights, minority protections), while taking a mixed stance on economic liberalism (calling for political primacy over unregulated markets and fair trade) and a mildly anti-liberal stance on financial markets (supporting a financial transaction tax).
