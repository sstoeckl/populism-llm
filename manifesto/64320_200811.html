WarningHow to read this page

The scores are holistic judgements. Each model read the whole manifesto text (in chunks of up to 20,000 tokens) and rated every dimension from the text as a whole. The passages quoted further down are examples the model cited, not the basis of the score: a score that rests on two paragraphs cannot be reduced to a few words, and a quoted passage alone will often look too weak, or too strong, for the number next to it. Read the models’ own explanations first; they state the reasoning behind each block of scores. The quotes then show where in the text the models looked.

Populism, populist ideology and liberalism

Thirteen dimensions scored on 0–10 by three model families from the full manifesto text, each with a verbatim evidence quote. Scores are aggregated as in Rode & Stöckl (2026): a chunk counts if the model marks its evidence as verified and the quote is found verbatim in the manifesto chunk (and is not copied from the prompt); confidence-weighted mean within model, plain mean across models.

What the models say about this manifesto

Each model’s own explanation of its scores, one per block of dimensions, written together with the scores.

Populism

Sonnet

The speech contains mild populist elements — framing financial elites as "greed merchants" who harm "ordinary people," and invoking "all our people" as a unified group — but the dominant register is technocratic and policy-focused, with no sustained Manichean narrative or strong people-vs-elite dichotomy.

GPT-4.1-mini

The manifesto criticizes financial elites as greedy and unregulated, appeals to New Zealanders as a unified group needing trustworthy leadership, and contrasts Labour's experience against opponents, indicating moderate populist themes.

Gemini

The text exhibits moderate populism by contrasting the 'greed' of international financial elites with the struggles of 'ordinary people' in real jobs.

Populist ideology

Sonnet

The populist flavour is predominantly left-leaning, with anti-privatisation stances, redistribution through Working for Families, opposition to financial deregulation, and framing of corporate financiers as the enemy of ordinary workers, though the overall tone is more social-democratic than strongly populist-left.

GPT-4.1-mini

Labour's focus on retraining allowances and support for workers shows left-leaning populist ideology, while appeals to national trust reflect centrist populism; no right-wing populist themes are evident.

Gemini

The populist rhetoric is strongly left-wing, focusing on anti-corporate sentiment, public ownership, and preventing the exploitation of the many by the few.

Liberalism

Sonnet

The manifesto supports democratic institutions, human rights, and rule of law (pro-liberal politically and socially), but takes a mixed economic stance — backing free trade agreements and R&D incentives while opposing deregulation and financial market liberalisation, and favouring state intervention in banking, health, and infrastructure.

GPT-4.1-mini

The manifesto strongly supports political rights and justice reforms, social protections including health system defense, economic incentives for R&D, and prudent financial market interventions, reflecting a pro-liberal stance overall.

Gemini

The text supports human rights, social inclusion, and state-supported market innovation, but strongly opposes financial deregulation and healthcare privatization.
