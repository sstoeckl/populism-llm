WarningHow to read this page

The scores are holistic judgements. Each model read the whole manifesto text (in chunks of up to 20,000 tokens) and rated every dimension from the text as a whole. The passages quoted further down are examples the model cited, not the basis of the score: a score that rests on two paragraphs cannot be reduced to a few words, and a quoted passage alone will often look too weak, or too strong, for the number next to it. Read the models’ own explanations first; they state the reasoning behind each block of scores. The quotes then show where in the text the models looked.

Populism, populist ideology and liberalism

Thirteen dimensions scored on 0–10 by three model families from the full manifesto text, each with a verbatim evidence quote. Scores are aggregated as in Rode & Stöckl (2026): a chunk counts if the model marks its evidence as verified and the quote is found verbatim in the manifesto chunk (and is not copied from the prompt); confidence-weighted mean within model, plain mean across models.

Populism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto contains mild anti-elitist rhetoric targeting short-sighted politicians and private investors who sacrifice the environment, and occasional appeals to citizens' will, but the dominant tone is technocratic and policy-oriented rather than populist.

chunk 2 · The manifesto contains mild anti-corporate critique (companies exploiting resources and paying minimal wages) and frames the green economy as serving people and nature, but lacks strong people-vs-elite dichotomies, conspiracy framing, or sustained populist rhetoric; the dominant tone is technocratic and policy-oriented.

chunk 3 · The text is primarily a detailed policy manifesto (science, health, social policy, youth, culture) with minimal populist framing; the only mild anti-elite rhetoric appears in criticism of politically motivated demonization of healthcare workers, but there is no sustained people-vs-elite narrative or Manichean framing.

chunk 4 · The text is a policy-oriented manifesto focused on elderly rights, foreign policy, and diaspora engagement; it contains minimal populist framing — a brief reference to institutional incapacity and the will of the people — but no sustained anti-elite rhetoric or Manichean good-vs-evil narrative.

chunk 3 · The manifesto leans mildly left-populist through its emphasis on social justice, equal opportunities, anti-discrimination, support for marginalized groups, and workers' rights, but it is primarily a programmatic green/social-liberal platform rather than a strongly ideological populist one.

Liberalism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto supports democratic transparency, pluralism (including sexual orientation), and reduced administrative barriers for businesses, while also proposing state-directed banking obligations and progressive taxation, yielding a mixed but moderately pro-liberal overall profile.

chunk 2 · The manifesto strongly endorses rule of law, independent judiciary, media freedom, and equal rights regardless of ethnicity/gender/sexuality (high lib_political and lib_social), calls for energy market liberalization and support for SMEs (moderate lib_economic), and mentions favorable bank credit conditions for green businesses (moderate lib_financial_market), yielding an overall moderately high liberal score.

chunk 3 · The manifesto is strongly pro-liberal on social dimensions (explicit LGBT rights, anti-discrimination, gender equality, minority protections) and moderately pro-liberal politically (democracy, rule of law, youth participation) and economically (SME support, public-private partnership, market competition), with financial-market liberalism present only in the form of subsidized credit instruments for youth and agriculture.

chunk 4 · The manifesto is strongly pro-liberal across all dimensions: it champions democratic values and EU/NATO integration, prohibits age-based discrimination and protects civil rights, promotes market-based economic initiatives and foreign investment, and explicitly supports non-discriminatory access to micro-financing.
