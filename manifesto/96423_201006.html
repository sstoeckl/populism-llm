WarningHow to read this page

The scores are holistic judgements. Each model read the whole manifesto text (in chunks of up to 20,000 tokens) and rated every dimension from the text as a whole. The passages quoted further down are examples the model cited, not the basis of the score: a score that rests on two paragraphs cannot be reduced to a few words, and a quoted passage alone will often look too weak, or too strong, for the number next to it. Read the models’ own explanations first; they state the reasoning behind each block of scores. The quotes then show where in the text the models looked.

Populism, populist ideology and liberalism

Thirteen dimensions scored on 0–10 by three model families from the full manifesto text, each with a verbatim evidence quote. Scores are aggregated as in Rode & Stöckl (2026): a chunk counts if the model marks its evidence as verified and the quote is found verbatim in the manifesto chunk (and is not copied from the prompt); confidence-weighted mean within model, plain mean across models.

What the models say about this manifesto

Each model’s own explanation of its scores, one per block of dimensions, written together with the scores.

Populism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto invokes the people as the central beneficiary of government policy and frames the political contest as a moral divide between left solidarity and right individualism/egoism, but stops short of strong elite-corruption rhetoric or conspiratorial Manichaeism, yielding a moderate populism score.

chunk 2 · The text is primarily a technocratic government programme with only faint populist traces: citizens are invoked as the country's core strength and there is a brief reference to past wrongs done to citizens, but there is no sustained elite-bashing, no Manichean good-vs-evil framing, and no unified "pure people" rhetoric.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto explicitly critiques the right-wing elite's governance and emphasizes a mandate to enact policies for the people through social justice and solidarity, reflecting moderate populist anti-elitism, people-centrism, and moral framing.

chunk 2 · The manifesto contains no explicit references to populist themes such as anti-elitism, people-centrism, or Manichean moral struggles, indicating absence of populism.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text exhibits moderate populism by framing the political struggle as a conflict between the 'people-centric' social democratic government and the 'deceitful' right-wing opposition.

chunk 2 · The text contains no populist rhetoric, anti-elitist framing, or moralized good-versus-evil divisions.

Populist ideology

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto is clearly left-populist: it champions the social state, redistribution, solidarity, and worker protection while explicitly criticising neoliberal/neoconservative right-wing parties; right-wing or centrist populist elements are minimal.

chunk 2 · The programme leans mildly left-populist through its emphasis on social solidarity, welfare-state protection, and workers' rights, while also carrying a conservative-national streak (Christian traditions, patriotism, traditional family); overall the ideological populist signal is weak and mixed.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The text clearly delineates left-wing support for state intervention and social welfare, right-wing market emphasis, and centrist positioning, showing a balanced populist ideological stance with a left-leaning emphasis.

chunk 2 · There is no evidence in the text of populist ideological positions on the left, right, or centrist populism; the manifesto focuses on policy and institutional frameworks without populist rhetoric.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The populist ideology is strongly left-wing, focusing heavily on state intervention, solidarity, and social justice against neoliberal market individualism.

chunk 2 · There is no evidence of populist ideology as the text focuses on standard policy proposals without populist framing.

Liberalism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto supports rule of law and anti-extremism (moderately pro-liberal politically and socially), but favours strong state intervention in the economy and mixed financial-sector regulation, producing a middling overall liberalism score.

chunk 2 · The manifesto explicitly endorses EU liberal-democratic values (rule of law, human rights, non-discrimination, gender equality) and supports market competition and financial-market stability, while also advocating a strong social state and regulated labour market, yielding a moderately pro-liberal overall profile.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto advocates for rule of law, social protections, coordinated economic policies, and financial market regulation that enable market function, demonstrating a pro-liberal stance across political, social, economic, and financial dimensions.

chunk 2 · The manifesto explicitly affirms commitment to liberal democratic values including democracy, rule of law, human rights, social equality, economic coordination, and financial market stability, demonstrating a strong pro-liberal stance.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text supports a social-market economy and the rule of law, but advocates for strong state intervention and financial sector regulation over pure classical liberalism.

chunk 2 · The text strongly supports democratic institutions, rule of law, gender equality, and small business development, while advocating for moderate regulation of financial markets.
