WarningHow to read this page

The scores are holistic judgements. Each model read the whole manifesto text (in chunks of up to 20,000 tokens) and rated every dimension from the text as a whole. The passages quoted further down are examples the model cited, not the basis of the score: a score that rests on two paragraphs cannot be reduced to a few words, and a quoted passage alone will often look too weak, or too strong, for the number next to it. Read the models’ own explanations first; they state the reasoning behind each block of scores. The quotes then show where in the text the models looked.

Populism, populist ideology and liberalism

Thirteen dimensions scored on 0–10 by three model families from the full manifesto text, each with a verbatim evidence quote. Scores are aggregated as in Rode & Stöckl (2026): a chunk counts if the model marks its evidence as verified and the quote is found verbatim in the manifesto chunk (and is not copied from the prompt); confidence-weighted mean within model, plain mean across models.

What the models say about this manifesto

Each model’s own explanation of its scores, one per block of dimensions, written together with the scores.

Populism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto shows moderate populist features: it invokes citizen participation and criticises entrenched privileges and 'rendite di posizione', but frames these critiques in programmatic, policy-oriented language rather than a sharp people-vs-elite moral binary.

chunk 2 · The text shows moderate populist elements: elites (the powerful, Berlusconi's right-wing government) are criticized as corrupt and self-serving, but the rhetoric is primarily policy-focused rather than a sustained Manichean people-vs-elite narrative.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto emphasizes broad citizen participation and transparency in politics, critiques elite privileges, and addresses xenophobia, reflecting moderate populist themes.

chunk 2 · The manifesto explicitly critiques political and economic elites and highlights a political crisis, invoking the people and moral contrasts in justice, indicating moderate populist themes.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text exhibits moderate populism by attacking the privileges of the political 'casta' and advocating for direct citizen participation.

chunk 2 · The text exhibits low overall populism, focusing on democratic participation and criticizing elite corruption and 'laws ad personam' without a dominant moralizing good-versus-evil narrative.

Populist ideology

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto is clearly left-leaning populist, emphasising wealth redistribution, progressive taxation, labour rights, and social equality, while explicitly opposing right-wing immigration restrictions (Bossi-Fini) and showing no nativist or identity-based populist appeals.

chunk 2 · The manifesto is clearly left-populist: it advocates redistribution, welfare expansion, ecological conversion, workers' rights, and anti-austerity policies, while explicitly rejecting right-wing xenophobic populism and national-identity politics.

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The text supports left-leaning populist ideas like social rights and tax justice, rejects right-wing immigration laws, and promotes centrist people-elite participation appeals.

chunk 2 · The text combines left-leaning social justice and redistribution themes with critiques of right-wing xenophobia and centrist institutional critiques, showing a mixed but left-leaning populist ideology.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The populist ideology is strongly left-wing, focusing on wealth redistribution through a wealth tax on financial assets and defending labor rights.

chunk 2 · The populist ideology is strongly left-wing, emphasizing wealth redistribution, public services, and ecological conversion while explicitly rejecting right-wing xenophobic nationalism.

Liberalism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto scores moderately on political and social liberalism (democratic participation, LGBTQ+ rights, minority protections), but is economically interventionist (public investment, welfare expansion, anti-austerity) and explicitly anti-free-market in finance (Tobin tax, opposition to unregulated financial markets), yielding a mixed overall liberal profile.

chunk 2 · The manifesto is strongly pro-liberal on political and social dimensions (defending judicial independence, checks and balances, LGBTQ+ rights, minority protections), but leans anti-liberal economically (opposing privatizations, supporting financial transaction taxes and state intervention in transport and banking).

GPT-4.1-mini

chunk 1 · The manifesto strongly supports democratic participation, civil rights, fair taxation, and investor protection, indicating a pro-liberal stance overall.

chunk 2 · The manifesto supports democratic institutions, civil rights including LGBTQ+ protections, equitable fiscal policies, and reforms in financial governance, reflecting a pro-liberal stance overall.

Gemini

chunk 1 · The text is highly progressive on social and political rights, but opposes free financial markets by supporting transaction taxes and bans on speculative instruments.

chunk 2 · The text strongly supports political and social liberalism through judicial independence and LGBTQ+ rights, but rejects economic and financial liberalism by opposing privatizations and advocating for financial transaction taxes.
