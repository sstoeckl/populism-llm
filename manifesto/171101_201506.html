WarningHow to read this page

The scores are holistic judgements. Each model read the whole manifesto text (in chunks of up to 20,000 tokens) and rated every dimension from the text as a whole. The passages quoted further down are examples the model cited, not the basis of the score: a score that rests on two paragraphs cannot be reduced to a few words, and a quoted passage alone will often look too weak, or too strong, for the number next to it. Read the models’ own explanations first; they state the reasoning behind each block of scores. The quotes then show where in the text the models looked.

Thirteen dimensions scored on 0–10 by three model families from the full manifesto text, each with a verbatim evidence quote. Scores are aggregated as in Rode & Stöckl (2026): a chunk counts if the model marks its evidence as verified and the quote is found verbatim in the manifesto chunk (and is not copied from the prompt); confidence-weighted mean within model, plain mean across models.

Liberalism

Sonnet

chunk 1 · The manifesto endorses democracy and citizen participation in political governance, non-discrimination in environmental rights, and uses fiscal incentives and market mechanisms (e.g., carbon markets, tax incentives) alongside regulatory mandates, reflecting a mixed but moderately liberal economic stance; financial market policy is entirely absent.

chunk 2 · The manifesto supports democratic participation, transparency, and rule of law in environmental governance; it advocates for inclusion of minorities and indigenous communities; and it shows mixed economic liberalism — supporting market access for forestry producers while also proposing subsidies and state regulation, with no discussion of financial markets at all.

chunk 3 · The manifesto consistently supports democratic accountability, transparency, gender equality, market competition, central bank autonomy, and open trade, placing it clearly in the pro-liberal range across all four sub-dimensions, though tempered by calls for environmental regulation and social spending.

chunk 4 · The manifesto shows moderate liberal tendencies: it supports transparency, democratic accountability, gender equality, and international trade/competitiveness, but also calls for state intervention in interest rates, import controls on GMOs, and increased regulation of agriculture and fisheries, producing a mixed overall liberal profile.
