Methodology

How the scores are built (data version v2026a)

🚧 PREVIEW / DRAFT · Working paper, not peer-reviewed. Numbers and methodology may change. Do not cite — sebastian.stoeckl@uni.li.

Overview

Corpus and chunks

The corpus is the Manifesto Project Dataset version 2026a with corpus release 2026-1: 3,371 manifestos with machine-readable text from 62 countries, 1945–2025. A manifesto of at most 18,000 tokens is sent whole; longer ones are split into chunks of 20,000 tokens with a 2,000-token overlap. The texts are © their authors and distributed by the Manifesto Project (WZB); this site does not redistribute them.

Models

Every chunk is scored by three model families with the same prompt and schema at temperature 0. The version actually served is logged for every call since October 2026; for the June 2026 calls it was not logged. The Gemini June rows used the floating alias gemini-flash-latest, which an anchor test identifies as gemini-3.5-flash.

What is scored

Per chunk, each model returns for each of thirteen dimensions a score from 0 to 10 (or none), an evidence status (verified, mixed or no evidence) and a verbatim quote of 3 to 8 words with its surrounding passage.

Group Dimension Range Definition
Populism Populism (overall) 0 to 10 Rounded mean of the three populism sub-dimensions, adjusted by one point for breadth.
Populism Anti-elitism 0 to 10 Elites or the establishment framed as corrupt or out of touch.
Populism People-centrism 0 to 10 ‘The people’ invoked as a unified, virtuous group.
Populism Manichaean outlook 0 to 10 Moralised good-versus-evil struggle; betrayal, conspiracy.
Populist ideology Populist ideology (strength) 0 to 10 Overall strength of the populist ideological flavour (not its direction).
Populist ideology Ideology: left 0 to 10 Anti-corporate elite, redistribution, social equality.
Populist ideology Ideology: right 0 to 10 National or identity protectionism, anti-immigration.
Populist ideology Ideology: centrist 0 to 10 Generic people–elite appeals without a clear programmatic stance.
Liberalism Liberalism (overall) 0 to 10 Rounded mean of the four liberalism sub-dimensions with evidence.
Liberalism Political liberalism 0 to 10 Democracy, rule of law, checks and balances, media freedom, due process.
Liberalism Social liberalism 0 to 10 Civil rights, minority protection, gender and LGBTQ+ equality, pluralism.
Liberalism Economic liberalism 0 to 10 Free markets, competition, enterprise, deregulation, tax efficiency, trade openness.
Liberalism Financial-market liberalism 0 to 10 Market pricing of assets and credit, open banking and capital markets, enabling prudential rules.
Populist ideology Ideology direction (right − left) -1 to 1 (R − L) / (R + L + C) on the cross-model means of the right, left and centrist scores; −1 left, +1 right.

From chunk scores to manifesto scores

  1. Which chunks count. A chunk’s score enters if the model rates its evidence as verified and the quote passes the quote check: it is found verbatim in the chunk and is not copied from the prompt. Every manifesto page shows each quote and whether it counted.
  2. Within a model: the mean of the counted chunk scores, weighted by the model’s confidence for that block of dimensions (populism, ideology or liberalism).
  3. Across models: the plain mean of the available models. This is the score shown as combined and used in the paper.
  4. Ideology direction: (right − left) / (right + left + centrist) on the combined scores, from −1 (left) to +1 (right).

The site recomputes these scores from the public files and checks that they equal the replication chain’s values exactly before any page is written.

Robustness

Every defensible alternative (model subsets, within- and between-model aggregation, evidence rules, samples, fixed effects, standard errors) is estimated; the distribution of the populism coefficient over all specifications is the specification curve. Results: the Milei question.

Evidence on this site

Quotes are shown with their first 8 words and without the surrounding passage, to respect the Manifesto Project’s terms of use. The full model output, including longer quotes and passages, is archived with the replication package and is not public.
